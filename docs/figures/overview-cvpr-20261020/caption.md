# Proposed figure caption

**Overview of DriveCompose.** Multi-camera driving observations are encoded into shared geometric, identity and state evidence. Background, rigid vehicles and articulated humans are organized in their respective world or canonical coordinate systems, while sky queries use a direction domain. Typed latent encoding provides a common interface for joint conditional completion. Local query groups exchange scene information through global memory; observed latent codes remain fixed and known free space is suppressed during integration and Gaussian decoding. Candidate content is checked in the original observed views before forming a fixed composable Gaussian world. “Ref.” groups the original images and the observed-only decoded reference used by this check. Runtime states control camera placement, rigid vehicle transforms and human skinning, allowing the same world content to be reused across multiple trajectories. External policy and dynamics update the state. Blue denotes observed-supported content, amber denotes completed content, gray denotes unknown support, and hatched cells denote free space. All scene depictions are mechanism illustrations.
