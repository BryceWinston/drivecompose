# 总览图图注

English: **DriveCompose overview.** (a) Calibrated images provide geometry, identity, and motion evidence, which is aggregated on background and canonical asset supports and encoded into structured latents. (b) A scene-conditioned flow transformer completes unknown codes while retaining observed content and suppressing free space. (c) Typed decoders produce Gaussian components. A check in the original views precedes scene reuse; camera and actor states then control composition and rendering. Scene depictions are schematic, not experimental outputs.

中文：**DriveCompose 总览。**（a）从有标定图像恢复几何、身份与运动证据，在背景和规范资产支撑上聚合并编码为结构化潜码。（b）场景条件流 Transformer 补全未知潜码，同时保留已见内容并抑制自由空间。（c）类型解码器输出高斯组件，在原始视角下检查后复用场景；相机与资产状态控制后续装配和渲染。图中场景为机制示意，不是实验输出。
