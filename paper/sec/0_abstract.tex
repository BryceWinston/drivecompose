\begin{abstract}
Reconstructing complete and controllable 3D scenes from partial observations is essential for autonomous driving simulation and scene editing. We present DriveCompose, a framework for generative reconstruction of compositional driving scenes from image sequences. Visual features are aggregated in world coordinates for the background and canonical frames for rigid assets and humans, then encoded into component-specific latent representations. A conditional generative model trained with flow matching uses cross-component memory to share scene context and jointly complete the missing codes in these representations. Completion is constrained by the input observations to preserve the geometry and appearance of observed surfaces. On Waymo, DriveCompose improves dynamic scene reconstruction and off-trajectory view synthesis over the evaluated baselines. Type-specific decoders convert the completed latents into Gaussian components with rigid and skeletal bindings, allowing the same assets to be composed and rendered under different camera and actor states.
\end{abstract}
