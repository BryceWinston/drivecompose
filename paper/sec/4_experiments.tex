\section{Experiments}
\label{sec:experiments}
We evaluate our method along three axes:
\textit{(i)} dynamic scene reconstruction quality on Waymo Open Dataset
(\S~\ref{sec:temporal_evaluation});
\textit{(ii)} off-trajectory view synthesis under lateral
camera shifts (\S~\ref{sec:spatial_evaluation}); and
\textit{(iii)} ablations of the main design choices
(\S~\ref{sec:ablations}).
Additional cross-dataset generalization results are provided in the supplementary material.

\subsection{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:experimental_setup}

\paragraph{Datasets and protocol.}
We use the Waymo Open Dataset with 798 training and 202 validation sequences. Following STORM, each validation sequence is divided into non-overlapping 2-second clips of 20 frames at 10 \, Hz~\cite{yang2025storm}. The FRONT, FRONT\_LEFT, and FRONT\_RIGHT cameras provide observations at zero-based frame indices $\{0,5,10,15\}$. The remaining 16 frames are used for evaluation: 12 frames for temporal interpolation and four frames for prediction beyond the last observation. Predictions and reference images are resized to $240\times160$ pixels for evaluation~\cite{huang2026instantnurec}.

\paragraph{Baselines.}
We compare with STORM, DGGT, StreetForward~\cite{yang2025storm,chen2025dggt,yu2026streetforward}. Methods use the same context timestamps and target views, with model-specific preprocessing. Camera parameters, additional geometric inputs, and rendering adapters are documented with the implementation settings. For spatial extrapolation, we compare Gaussian renderings before diffusion refinement, following PointForward~\cite{chi2026pointforward}.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
We report PSNR and SSIM over the full image and shared annotated regions. Depth quality is measured using AbsRel and $\delta_1$ at valid projected LiDAR samples after per-frame scale alignment~\cite{huang2026instantnurec}. Scores are averaged over frames within each clip and then over clips within each sequence; sequences receive equal weight. Novel-trajectory renderings are evaluated using FID and FVD against a fixed real-image and video reference population.

\paragraph{Implementation details.}
DriveCompose reconstructs category-specific actor assets from motion-aligned observations and composes them with a static background. Reconstruction time includes preprocessing, asset generation, and scene composition. Rendering time is measured separately, and asset reuse is evaluated across repeated scene queries.

\subsection{Dynamic Scene Reconstruction}
\label{sec:temporal_evaluation}

We evaluate reconstruction at unobserved times along the recorded camera trajectory. Table~\ref{tab:regions_current} reports full-image, dynamic-region, and depth quality; Fig.~\ref{fig:dynamic-reconstruction} compares rendered appearance across five examples.

\paragraph{Quantitative comparison.}
DriveCompose achieves the best scores across all six metrics in Table~\ref{tab:regions_current}. Full-image and dynamic-region PSNR exceed the strongest baseline in each category by 1.154 and 2.340 dB, respectively. The corresponding SSIM scores reach 0.901 and 0.862, while depth AbsRel decreases to 0.095 and $\delta_1$ reaches 0.877.

\paragraph{Qualitative comparison.}
Figure~\ref{fig:dynamic-reconstruction} shows that DriveCompose retains the pedestrian silhouette in the first row and the rear profile of the central vehicle in the second row, while preserving the surrounding street layout. The baseline renderings exhibit displaced or repeated actor contours in the upper rows and blurred or distorted road structure in the lower rows. DriveCompose recovers these reference structures more closely. Residual artifacts occur around the pedestrians in the third row and the parked pickup in the last row.

\subsection{Ablation studies}
\label{sec:ablation-protocol}
\label{sec:ablations}
\label{sec:ablation-studies}

\paragraph{Protocol.}
We evaluate both ablations on held-out Waymo images using a shared 29-timestamp window.
Each window uses three synchronized cameras: front, front-left, and front-right.
The nested input sets are $\mathcal T_2=\{0,28\}$, $\mathcal T_4=\{0,8,20,28\}$, and $\mathcal T_8=\{0,4,\ldots,28\}$, with shared target timestamps $\{2,6,\ldots,26\}$.
Target images are excluded from evidence and query construction; target camera and actor states are supplied equally to every variant.
The recovery module and codec are frozen, with matched query layouts, data exposure, and optimization budgets at each input setting.
Training samples $T\in\{2,4,8\}$ uniformly; completion uses 24 Euler steps.
The two learned variants use training seeds $\{0,1,2\}$ and world seeds $\{7,17,27\}$; the variant w/o GCM is deterministic.
Hyperparameters are selected on an internal development split before evaluation on scene- and identity-disjoint data.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
We report PSNR, SSIM, and LPIPS over the full held-out target images, with PSNR and SSIM additionally evaluated on dynamic regions for component ablations.
All variants use the same image preprocessing, evaluation resolution, and annotation-based dynamic-region masks as the main reconstruction experiment.
Metrics are computed on all renderable scenes before any output filtering; non-renderable scenes are counted separately.
Scores are averaged over frames within each window, then over windows within each sequence, and finally across sequences and training seeds with equal weight.

\paragraph{Completion and scene context.}
Table~\ref{tab:ablation-components} compares the full model with two controls.
The variant w/o GCM bypasses the generative completion module and disables the opacity of every non-observed, non-sky query.
The variant w/o CCM replaces the cross-component memory module with isolated per-component memories at every layer, while sharing parameters and retaining the same global log context.
The learned control is retrained through Stages 3--4; memory-related compute differences are recorded.
Ours achieves the best scores on all five metrics.
Compared with w/o GCM, it raises full-image and dynamic-region PSNR by 1.776 and 2.186 dB, respectively.
Relative to w/o CCM, the corresponding gains are 0.627 and 0.934 dB, while full-image LPIPS decreases from 0.147 to 0.129.

\subsection{State-driven reuse}
\label{sec:ablation-reuse}

At timestamp 14 and world seed 7, the interventions independently translate the camera by $1\,\mathrm m$ along its local $+x$ axis, rotate a vehicle by $30^\circ$ about its root and the world vertical axis, or apply a held-out valid human pose $H_1$ with fixed root placement.
Lighting and canonical assets remain fixed, without resampling completion.
