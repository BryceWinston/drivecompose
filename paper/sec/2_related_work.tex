\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Radiance representations.}
NeRF represents geometry and appearance through a continuous radiance field~\cite{mildenhall2020nerf}, with extensions for unconstrained photographs and multiscale sampling~\cite{martinbrualla2021nerfw,barron2021mipnerf}. Gaussian splatting provides explicit primitives for rendering~\cite{kerbl2023gaussians}; Mip-Splatting addresses aliasing~\cite{yu2024mipsplatting}, while Gaussian ray tracing and 3DGUT extend ray handling and camera models~\cite{moenne2024gaussianraytracing,wu2025gut}. D-NeRF and deformable Gaussians introduce time-dependent deformation~\cite{pumarola2021dnerf,yang2024deformablegs}. These representations establish the rendering machinery on which scene decomposition and asset control can be built.

\paragraph{Urban scenes and control.}
Urban Radiance Fields and Block-NeRF address large urban captures~\cite{rematas2022urban,tancik2022blocknerf}. NeuRAD, EVolSplat, and SplatAD adapt neural rendering to driving scenes and sensors~\cite{tonderski2024neurad,miao2025evolsplat,hess2025splatad}. Neural Scene Graphs and EmerNeRF organize dynamics through object structure or self-supervised decomposition~\cite{ost2021neuralgraphs,yang2024emernerf}. Street Gaussians, DrivingGaussian, and holistic HUGS distinguish foreground content from the background~\cite{yan2024street,zhou2024drivinggaussian,zhou2024holistichugs}; OmniRe extends reconstruction to diverse actor types~\cite{chen2025omnire}. UniSim and HUGSIM connect reconstructed scenes to closed-loop simulation~\cite{yang2023unisim,zhou2024hugsim}. We preserve component identity and motion rules within the variables used for generative completion.

\paragraph{Feed-forward reconstruction.}
LRM, LGM, and GS-LRM learn image-to-3D prediction~\cite{hong2024lrm,tang2024lgm,zhang2024gslrm}. pixelSplat, MVSplat, and DepthSplat recover Gaussians from sparse views~\cite{charatan2024pixelsplat,chen2024mvsplat,xu2025depthsplat}, while DUSt3R, MASt3R, and VGGT recover geometry and correspondences with transformers~\cite{wang2024dust3r,leroy2024mast3r,wang2025vggt}. Fast3R and CUT3R explore joint and recurrent processing of image collections~\cite{yang2025fast3r,wang2025cut3r}. TokenGS explicitly decouples Gaussian prediction from pixels through learnable tokens~\cite{ren2026tokengs}. Our distinction concerns the variables and inference task: conditional generation completes background and canonical asset codes before they are decoded into a reusable scene.

\paragraph{Driving-scene inference.}
STORM predicts dynamic Gaussians and aggregates them through scene flow~\cite{yang2025storm}. DrivingForward studies flexible surround-view inputs~\cite{tian2025drivingforward}, and DGGT combines pose-free reconstruction, temporal visibility, and motion-guided interpolation~\cite{chen2025dggt}. StreetForward, PointForward, and Instant NuRec investigate temporal processing, point-aligned representations, and simulation-oriented reconstruction~\cite{yu2026streetforward,chi2026pointforward,huang2026instantnurec}. DriveCompose uses recovered geometry and motion to gather evidence on persistent supports, where missing content can be completed before state changes expose it.

\paragraph{Canonical and animatable assets.}
SMPL and SMPL-X define articulated body models~\cite{loper2015smpl,pavlakos2019smplx}; Humans in 4D and WHAM recover human shape and motion from images~\cite{goel2023humans4d,shin2024wham}. HumanNeRF and Vid2Avatar reconstruct people from videos~\cite{weng2022humannerf,guo2023vid2avatar}, while GaussianAvatar, GauHuman, human HUGS, and 3DGS-Avatar use animatable Gaussians~\cite{hu2024gaussianavatar,hu2024gauhuman,kocabas2024hugs,qian2024avatar}. CanonicalFusion, FRESA, LHM, and IDOL infer drivable or animatable human assets from one or more images~\cite{shin2024canonicalfusion,wang2025fresa,qiu2025lhm,zhuang2025idol}. UrbanCAD instead targets controllable vehicle assets~\cite{lu2025urbancad}. Segmentation models and synthetic body data offer complementary supervision resources~\cite{kirillov2023sam,black2023bedlam}. We use category-specific supports to retain each asset's coordinates and motion binding throughout completion.

\paragraph{Generative reconstruction and completion.}
DreamFusion distills a 2D diffusion prior into 3D content~\cite{poole2023dreamfusion}; Zero-1-to-3, MVDream, and CAT3D generate novel or multiple views for 3D creation~\cite{liu2023zero123,shi2024mvdream,gao2024cat3d}. Objaverse provides explicit asset data for 3D learning~\cite{deitke2023objaverse}. GenAssets learns driving-asset priors~\cite{yang2025genassets}, and structured latent models jointly encode geometry and appearance for generation~\cite{xiang2025trellis,xiang2026trellis2}. CompleteSplat completes Gaussian scenes, JRM combines unaligned object observations through latent flow matching, and LatentWorld organizes semantic occupancy generation around entities~\cite{liao2025complete,wu2026jrm,park2026latentworld}. DriveCompose completes RGB-renderable background and canonical assets in a shared query layout, with observed codes and free space constraining the unknown content.

\paragraph{Controlled driving-video generation.}
MagicDrive, MagicDrive-V2, DriveDreamer, and Vista condition driving imagery on scene structure or control signals~\cite{gao2024magicdrive,gao2025magicdrivev2,wang2024drivedreamer,gao2024vista}. GEN3C uses 3D information for camera-controlled video generation~\cite{ren2025gen3c}. StreetCrafter and HorizonForge combine controllable diffusion with scene reconstruction or editing~\cite{streetcrafter,wang2026horizonforge}; WorldSplat generates Gaussians and refines their rendered videos~\cite{zhu2025worldsplat}, and DIFIX3D+ improves reconstructions with diffusion-based image refinement~\cite{wu2025difix3d}. Perceptual similarity measures such as LPIPS assess rendered appearance~\cite{zhang2018lpips}. Our formulation additionally assigns completed content a persistent identity, coordinate frame, and motion rule, so subsequent state changes reuse the same 3D completion.
