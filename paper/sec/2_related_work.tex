\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Driving-scene reconstruction.}
Urban reconstruction builds on NeRF and Gaussian radiance representations~\cite{mildenhall2020nerf,kerbl2023gaussians}, with driving-specific rendering developed in NeuRAD and SplatAD~\cite{tonderski2024neurad,hess2025splatad}. Feed-forward methods predict scene geometry and Gaussians directly from images~\cite{hong2024lrm,charatan2024pixelsplat,chen2024mvsplat,wang2024dust3r,wang2025vggt}. STORM aggregates dynamic Gaussians through scene flow, while DGGT combines camera recovery with visibility and motion-guided interpolation~\cite{yang2025storm,chen2025dggt}. Surround-view inputs, temporal processing, point-aligned representations, and simulation-oriented reconstruction are explored by DrivingForward, StreetForward, PointForward, and Instant NuRec~\cite{tian2025drivingforward,yu2026streetforward,chi2026pointforward,huang2026instantnurec}. TokenGS uses learnable tokens to decouple Gaussian prediction from pixels~\cite{ren2026tokengs}.

\paragraph{Compositional scene representations.}
Separating background and moving components supports actor control in Neural Scene Graphs, Street Gaussians, and DrivingGaussian~\cite{ost2021neuralgraphs,yan2024street,zhou2024drivinggaussian}. OmniRe represents rigid and articulated actors in canonical frames, while UniSim and HUGSIM connect reconstructed content to closed-loop simulation~\cite{chen2025omnire,yang2023unisim,zhou2024hugsim}. At the asset level, SMPL-based body models and animatable Gaussian methods provide canonical surfaces and motion bindings~\cite{loper2015smpl,pavlakos2019smplx,hu2024gauhuman,kocabas2024hugs,qian2024avatar}; LHM and IDOL infer animatable humans, and UrbanCAD targets controllable vehicles~\cite{qiu2025lhm,zhuang2025idol,lu2025urbancad}. These frames and bindings provide the interface for reusing surfaces after reconstruction or completion.

\paragraph{Generative reconstruction and completion.}
Generative reconstruction infers unobserved geometry and appearance through generated views~\cite{liu2023zero123,shi2024mvdream,gao2024cat3d} or structured latent representations~\cite{xiang2025trellis,xiang2026trellis2}. GenAssets learns priors for driving assets, CompleteSplat generates hidden Gaussian surfaces, and JRM couples object latents to aggregate unaligned observations~\cite{yang2025genassets,liao2025complete,wu2026jrm}. LatentWorld organizes semantic occupancy generation around entity and background latents~\cite{park2026latentworld}. DriveCompose jointly completes world-space background and canonical rigid and human codes, retaining their geometry, appearance, and motion bindings for state-driven rendering.

\paragraph{Driving-scene generation.}
MagicDrive, DriveDreamer, and Vista generate driving imagery conditioned on scene structure or control~\cite{gao2024magicdrive,wang2024drivedreamer,gao2024vista}. StreetCrafter uses generated views to supervise dynamic Gaussian reconstruction, and HorizonForge combines scene editing with video diffusion~\cite{streetcrafter,wang2026horizonforge}. WorldSplat generates Gaussians and refines their rendered videos, while DIFIX3D+ applies diffusion-based image refinement~\cite{zhu2025worldsplat,wu2025difix3d}. FreeGen co-trains feed-forward reconstruction with diffusion-based generation, distilling generative priors into reconstruction for off-trajectory rendering~\cite{chen2026freegen}. DriveCompose stores the completed background and canonical assets, then renders the same 3D content under new camera and actor states.
