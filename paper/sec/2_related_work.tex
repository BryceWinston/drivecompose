\section{Related work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Driving-scene reconstruction.}
NeRF and Gaussian splatting provide the radiance representations underlying urban reconstruction~\cite{mildenhall2020nerf,kerbl2023gaussians}, with driving-specific rendering developed in NeuRAD and SplatAD~\cite{tonderski2024neurad,hess2025splatad}. Feed-forward methods learn geometry and Gaussian prediction from images~\cite{hong2024lrm,charatan2024pixelsplat,chen2024mvsplat,wang2024dust3r,wang2025vggt}. For driving logs, STORM aggregates dynamic Gaussians through scene flow, while DGGT combines camera recovery with temporal visibility and motion-guided interpolation~\cite{yang2025storm,chen2025dggt}. DrivingForward, StreetForward, PointForward, and Instant NuRec further explore surround-view inputs, temporal processing, point-aligned representations, and simulation-oriented reconstruction~\cite{tian2025drivingforward,yu2026streetforward,chi2026pointforward,huang2026instantnurec}. TokenGS also decouples Gaussian prediction from pixels through learnable tokens~\cite{ren2026tokengs}.

\paragraph{Compositional scenes and controllable assets.}
Neural Scene Graphs, Street Gaussians, and DrivingGaussian separate scene content into background and moving components~\cite{ost2021neuralgraphs,yan2024street,zhou2024drivinggaussian}. OmniRe includes canonical representations for rigid and articulated actors, while UniSim and HUGSIM connect reconstructed content to closed-loop simulation~\cite{chen2025omnire,yang2023unisim,zhou2024hugsim}. At the asset level, SMPL-based body models and animatable Gaussian methods provide canonical surfaces and motion bindings~\cite{loper2015smpl,pavlakos2019smplx,hu2024gauhuman,kocabas2024hugs,qian2024avatar}; LHM and IDOL infer animatable humans, and UrbanCAD targets controllable vehicles~\cite{qiu2025lhm,zhuang2025idol,lu2025urbancad}. Our completion variables retain these established component identities, frames, and bindings.

\paragraph{Generative reconstruction and completion.}
Image-conditioned 3D generation uses generated views~\cite{liu2023zero123,shi2024mvdream,gao2024cat3d} or structured latents encoding geometry and appearance~\cite{xiang2025trellis,xiang2026trellis2}. GenAssets learns driving-asset priors, CompleteSplat completes hidden Gaussian content, and JRM aggregates unaligned object observations through latent flow matching~\cite{yang2025genassets,liao2025complete,wu2026jrm}. LatentWorld organizes semantic occupancy generation around entity and background latents~\cite{park2026latentworld}. DriveCompose targets RGB-renderable reconstruction conditioned on driving logs: a shared completion model exchanges context across world-space background and canonical rigid and human codes.

\paragraph{Generative driving scenes and videos.}
MagicDrive, DriveDreamer, and Vista generate driving imagery conditioned on scene structure or control~\cite{gao2024magicdrive,wang2024drivedreamer,gao2024vista}. StreetCrafter uses generated views to supervise dynamic Gaussian reconstruction; HorizonForge combines scene editing with video diffusion~\cite{streetcrafter,wang2026horizonforge}. WorldSplat generates Gaussians and refines their rendered videos, while DIFIX3D+ applies diffusion-based image refinement~\cite{zhu2025worldsplat,wu2025difix3d}. DriveCompose completes and stores background and canonical assets once, then renders them under new camera and actor states.
