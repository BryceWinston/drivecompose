\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

DriveCompose reconstructs finite regions under fixed illumination, using rigid vehicles and humans bound to a fixed skeleton and clothing volume. Depth, identity, and canonical-pose accuracy determine the observed anchors inherited by completion. Residual reconstruction artifacts occur near pedestrians and the parked pickup in Fig.~\ref{fig:dynamic-reconstruction}. Editing failures concentrate on large elbow bends, leg self-occlusion, and loose-clothing boundaries. Relighting and clothing-geometry changes call for richer appearance and deformation models.
