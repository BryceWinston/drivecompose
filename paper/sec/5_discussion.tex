\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

DriveCompose covers a finite region under fixed illumination. Its observation anchors inherit errors in recovered depth, identity, and canonical pose. Vehicles remain rigid; human geometry uses fixed clothing volume and skeleton binding, so poses outside the template's support can expose missing content. Reference-world coordinates describe the construction scene, while contact and collision validity after an asset moves are handled by the physical backend. The observed-view check constrains consistency with recorded evidence; geometry beyond that evidence and behavior under new states require their own assessment.
