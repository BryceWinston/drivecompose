\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented DriveCompose, which reconstructs compositional driving scenes through canonical feature aggregation, latent encoding, and joint completion. Local attention and cross-component memory combine component structure with scene context. Type-specific Gaussian decoders retain identity and motion bindings, allowing completed assets to be composed under changing camera and actor states. On Waymo, DriveCompose improves dynamic reconstruction and off-trajectory view synthesis.
